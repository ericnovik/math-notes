2  Schedule

60 weeks, ~5 sessions/week. Start: Mon 2026-06-01. End: Sun 2027-07-25.

The schedule is a target, not a contract. Real life slips; the right response is to shift the calendar, not to skip the daily handwriting session. Fluency, not speed.

2.1 Daily routine

Block Time What
Session 30–45 min Handwritten in the dedicated notebook. Read concept, work example, write definition.
Transfer 15–20 min, hours later Copy from _templates/concept.qmd, fill in cleanly, photograph notebook page into notebook/.

2.2 Weekly rhythm

Day Focus
Mon–Thu Primary text (current phase)
Fri Needham — Visual Complex Analysis
Sat Synthesis note — one paragraph tying the week’s concepts together
Sun Off

Low-energy day swap: Strogatz (Nonlinear Dynamics) or Mahajan (Street-Fighting Mathematics) in place of the primary text. Don’t skip; substitute.

2.3 Phase 1 — Linear Algebra (Axler), 15 weeks

Goal: formalize and sharpen what’s already intuitive from applied work. First phase, lower cognitive load, locks in the daily habit before analysis gets hard.

Weeks Dates Material
1–3 Jun 1 – Jun 21, 2026 Axler Ch 1 — Vector spaces
4–5 Jun 22 – Jul 5 Axler Ch 2 — Finite-dimensional vector spaces, bases, dimension
6–7 Jul 6 – Jul 19 Axler Ch 3A — Linear maps, null space, range
8–10 Jul 20 – Aug 9 Axler Ch 3B–4 — Matrices, invertibility, polynomials
11–13 Aug 10 – Aug 30 Axler Ch 5 — Eigenvalues, eigenvectors, invariant subspaces
14–15 Aug 31 – Sep 13 Axler Ch 6 — Inner product spaces; review week

Cross-reference: Strang for geometric matrix pictures; Lang when you want the algebraic generalization.

2.4 Phase 2 — Real Analysis (Abbott), 20 weeks

Goal: ε–δ and limit machinery that Rosenthal assumes. The proof fluency from Axler carries over.

Weeks Dates Material
16–20 Sep 14 – Oct 18, 2026 Abbott Ch 1 — Real numbers, completeness, cardinality
21–25 Oct 19 – Nov 22 Abbott Ch 2 — Sequences and series
26–30 Nov 23, 2026 – Dec 27, 2026 Abbott Ch 3 — Topology of ℝ
31–35 Dec 28, 2026 – Jan 31, 2027 Abbott Ch 4 — Functional limits, continuity

2.5 Phase 3 — Measure-Theoretic Probability (Rosenthal), 25 weeks

The original goal. Direct payoff for Bayesian modeling, sequential trials, conditional expectation as the right way to think about posteriors.

Weeks Dates Material
36–39 Feb 1 – Feb 28, 2027 Rosenthal Ch 1 — Probability triples, σ-algebras
40–43 Mar 1 – Mar 28 Rosenthal Ch 2 — Extension of measures
44–48 Mar 29 – May 2 Rosenthal Ch 3 — Random variables, expected value, Lebesgue integral
49–53 May 3 – Jun 6 Rosenthal Ch 4 — Convergence theorems (MCT, DCT, Fatou)
54–57 Jun 7 – Jul 4 Rosenthal Ch 5 — Independence
58–60 Jul 5 – Jul 25 Rosenthal Ch 6 — Conditional expectation

After Phase 3: martingales (Williams) → optional stopping → sequential trial design. Or Radon-Nikodym → likelihood as a density. Pick at the time based on what’s pulling.

2.6 Parallel tracks

Run alongside the phase work, no fixed schedule beyond “Friday by default.”

  • Needham — Visual Complex Analysis. Chs 1–5 to start. Geometric instinct, low activation energy.
  • Osgood — Lectures on the Fourier Transform and Its Applications. Stanford EE 261. Intuition + rigor, signal-processing applications, distributions and sampling. Friday substitute when Needham isn’t pulling.
  • Strogatz — Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos. Browse-driven; pick a chapter when curious.
  • Mahajan — Street-Fighting Mathematics. One problem per low-energy session. Counterweight to formalism.
